# One-on-One Communication Skill

## Overview
The ability to conduct effective individual conversations for mentoring, feedback, coaching, and relationship building.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Active Listening
- **Full Attention**: Eliminate distractions, maintain eye contact
- **Reflective Listening**: Paraphrase to confirm understanding
- **Non-Verbal Cues**: Read body language and tone
- **Silence Comfort**: Allow pauses for thought
- **Clarifying Questions**: Dig deeper without interrogation

### 2. Empathy & Emotional Intelligence
- **Perspective Taking**: See situations from their viewpoint
- **Emotion Recognition**: Identify underlying feelings
- **Validation**: Acknowledge experiences without judgment
- **Appropriate Response**: Match emotional tone appropriately

### 3. Feedback Delivery
- **Specificity**: Concrete examples, not generalizations
- **Timeliness**: Close to the event, but right moment
- **Balance**: Positive and constructive elements
- **Actionability**: Clear path forward
- **Two-Way**: Invite their perspective and questions

### 4. Coaching & Mentoring
- **Goal Setting**: Help define clear objectives
- **Guided Discovery**: Ask vs. tell approach
- **Accountability**: Follow-up on commitments
- **Growth Mindset**: Focus on development potential
- **Resource Sharing**: Provide relevant tools and connections

## Frameworks & Methods

### SBI Feedback Model
- **Situation**: Describe the specific context
- **Behavior**: State the observable action (not interpretation)
- **Impact**: Explain the effect on you/team/project

**Example:**
> "In yesterday's standup (Situation), when you interrupted Sarah twice while she was speaking (Behavior), I noticed the team became quieter and she didn't share her update (Impact)."

### GROW Coaching Model
1. **Goal**: What do you want to achieve?
2. **Reality**: What's happening now?
3. **Options**: What could you do?
4. **Will/Way Forward**: What will you do?

### Radical Candor Framework
```
                    Challenge Directly
                           ↑
                           |
              Ruinous      |      Obnoxious
              Empathy      |      Aggression
                           |
 ← Care Personally ────────┼─────────────── → Indifference
                           |
              Manipulative |      Healthy
              Insincerity  |      Relationship
                           |
                           ↓
                    Avoid Confrontation

Target: Top-right quadrant (Care Personally + Challenge Directly)
```

## Practical Templates

### One-on-One Meeting Template
```markdown
# 1:1 Agenda - [Name] - [Date]

## Check-in (5 min)
- How are you doing personally?
- Energy level: 1-10
- Any wins to celebrate?

## Review Previous Action Items (5 min)
- [ ] Item 1: Status?
- [ ] Item 2: Status?
- [ ] Item 3: Status?

## Their Topics (15 min)
- What's on your mind?
- Any blockers or challenges?
- What would you like to discuss?

## My Topics (10 min)
- Feedback/observations
- Project updates
- Career development discussion

## Growth & Development (10 min)
- Progress on goals
- Learning opportunities
- Skills to develop

## Action Items (5 min)
- [ ] They will: ...
- [ ] I will: ...
- [ ] We will: ...

## Notes
[Any additional observations or follow-ups]
```

## Common Pitfalls

### ❌ What to Avoid
- Multitasking during conversations
- Interrupting before they finish
- Jumping to solutions immediately
- Making it about yourself
- Giving vague or generic feedback
- Saving all feedback for formal reviews
- Dominating the conversation
- Ignoring non-verbal signals
- Being inconsistent with follow-through
- Having agenda-only meetings without relationship building

### ✅ Best Practices
- Schedule recurring 1:1s and protect them
- Let them set the agenda when possible
- Take notes and reference previous conversations
- Start with positive observations
- Ask open-ended questions
- Listen 70%, speak 30%
- Follow up on commitments promptly
- Create psychological safety
- Be vulnerable and authentic
- End with clear next steps

## Example Application

### Scenario: Addressing Performance Concerns

**Preparation:**
- Gather specific examples of concerning behavior
- Consider potential root causes (skill gap, motivation, personal issues)
- Plan supportive approach, not punitive

**Opening:**
> "I wanted to talk about something I've noticed because I care about your success here. I've observed [specific behavior] in [specific situations], and I'm concerned about the impact on [team/project]. Can we explore what's going on?"

**During Conversation:**
- Listen to their perspective fully
- Ask: "What's your take on this?"
- Explore: "What obstacles are you facing?"
- Support: "How can I help?"

**Action Plan:**
- Co-create improvement plan
- Set clear expectations
- Schedule frequent check-ins
- Offer resources and support

**Follow-up:**
- Acknowledge improvements quickly
- Adjust support as needed
- Document progress

## Success Indicators
- Team members seek you out for conversations
- Open sharing of challenges and concerns
- Visible growth and development over time
- Strong trust and psychological safety
- Proactive problem-solving by team members
- Positive feedback in anonymous surveys
- Retention and engagement metrics improve
