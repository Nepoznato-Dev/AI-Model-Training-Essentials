Leadership, people management, and strategic decision-making for technical leaders.

## Why Management Skills Matter

Effective management enables:
- Clear direction and goals
- High-performing teams
- Efficient resource allocation
- Risk mitigation
- Career growth for team members
- Better work-life balance for the team
- Increased productivity and morale
