## Literary Genres 와 Forms

### Fiction Genres
- **Literary Fiction**: Character-driven narratives focusing on themes, style, 와 psychological depth (e.g., "To Kill a Mockingbird" by Harper Lee)
- **과학 Fiction**: Speculative fiction exploring futuristic 기술, space exploration, time travel, parallel universes (e.g., "Dune" by Frank Herbert, "1984" by George Orwell)
- **Fantasy**: Magical worlds, mythical creatures, supernatural elements (e.g., "그 Lord 의 그 Rings" by J.R.R. Tolkien, "Harry Potter" by J.K. Rowling)
- **Mystery/Detective**: Crime-solving narratives 와 함께 puzzles 와 investigations (e.g., Agatha Christie's Hercule Poirot series, Arthur Conan Doyle's Sherlock Holmes)
- **Horror**: Intended to frighten, unsettle, or create suspense (e.g., "그 Shining" by Stephen King, "Dracula" by Bram Stoker)
- **Romance**: Focus on romantic relationships 와 emotional journeys (e.g., Jane Austen's "Pride 와 Prejudice", Nicholas Sparks novels)
- **Historical Fiction**: Set 에서 specific historical periods 와 함께 accurate details (e.g., "그 Book Thief" by Markus Zusak, "Wolf Hall" by Hilary Mantel)
- **Magical Realism**: Realistic settings 와 함께 magical elements treated as ordinary (e.g., "One Hundred Years 의 Solitude" by Gabriel García Márquez)

### Non-Fiction Genres
- **Biography/Autobiography**: Life stories 의 real people (e.g., "그 Diary 의 a Young Girl" by Anne Frank, "Becoming" by Michelle Obama)
- **Memoir**: Personal accounts focusing on specific experiences or themes
- **Essay**: Short prose compositions on particular subjects (e.g., works by James Baldwin, Joan Didion)
- **Journalism**: News reporting, investigative pieces, feature articles
- **Academic Writing**: Research papers, scholarly articles, dissertations
- **Self-Help**: Personal 개발 와 improvement guides

### Poetry Forms
- **Sonnet**: 14-line poem 와 함께 specific rhyme scheme (Shakespearean: ABAB CDCD EFEF GG; Petrarchan: ABBAABBA CDECDE)
- **Haiku**: Japanese form 와 함께 5-7-5 syllable pattern
- **Limerick**: Five-line humorous poem 와 함께 AABBA rhyme scheme
- **Free Verse**: No regular meter or rhyme pattern
- **Villanelle**: 19 lines 와 함께 specific repetition pattern (e.g., "Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night" by Dylan Thomas)
- **Ode**: Lyrical poem praising a person, event, or thing
- **Epic**: Long narrative poem about heroic deeds (e.g., "그 Odyssey" by Homer, "Beowulf")

## Major Literary Movements

### Historical Periods
- **Classical 문학** (8th century BCE - 5th century CE): Greek 와 Roman works emphasizing reason, order, 와 humanism
- **Medieval 문학** (5th - 15th century): Religious themes, chivalric romances, allegories (e.g., "그 Divine Comedy" by Dante)
- **Renaissance** (14th - 17th century): Humanism, revival 의 classical learning (e.g., Shakespeare, Cervantes)
- **Enlightenment** (17th - 18th century): Reason, 과학, individual rights (e.g., Voltaire, Jonathan Swift)
- **Romanticism** (late 18th - mid-19th century): Emotion, 자연, individualism (e.g., Wordsworth, Goethe, Mary Shelley)
- **Realism** (mid-19th century): Accurate depiction 의 everyday life (e.g., Tolstoy, George Eliot, Mark Twain)

## Visual 예술

## Music

### Classical Music Periods
- **Medieval** (500-1400): Gregorian chant, early polyphony
- **Renaissance** (1400-1600): Vocal polyphony, madrigals (e.g., Palestrina, Josquin des Prez)

- **Notes 와 Scales**: Major, minor, chromatic, pentatonic, modes

## Literary Devices 와 Techniques

### Figurative 언어
- **Metaphor**: Direct comparison without "like" or "as"
- **Simile**: Comparison using "like" or "as"
- **Personification**: Giving human qualities to non-human things
- **Hyperbole**: Deliberate exaggeration
- **Irony**: Contrast between expectation 와 reality (verbal, situational, dramatic)
- **Symbolism**: Objects representing abstract ideas
- **Imagery**: Descriptive 언어 appealing to senses

### Narrative Techniques
- **Point 의 View**: First person, second person, third person (limited, omniscient)
- **Stream 의 Consciousness**: Continuous flow 의 thoughts
- **Unreliable Narrator**: Narrator whose credibility is compromised
- **Non-linear Narrative**: 이벤트 presented out 의 chronological order
- **Foreshadowing**: Hints about 미래 이벤트
- **Flashback**: Scenes from earlier 에서 그 story

### Structural Elements
- **Plot**: Exposition, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution
- **Character 개발**: Protagonist, antagonist, dynamic/static characters
- **Setting**: Time, place, social context
- **Theme**: Central message or underlying meaning
- **Tone**: Author's attitude toward subject
- **Mood**: Emotional atmosphere created 위한 reader

## Key Concepts 에서 Art Criticism

### Analysis Frameworks
- **Formalism**: Focus on visual elements (line, color, composition)
- **Iconography**: Symbolic meanings 와 cultural references

- **Feminist Criticism**: Gender representation, women artists

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Balance**: Visual weight distribution (symmetrical, asymmetrical)
- **Contrast**: Differences 에서 elements (light/dark, large/small)
- **Emphasis**: Focal points, areas 의 interest
- **Movement**: Visual flow guiding viewer's eye
- **Pattern**: Repetition 의 elements
- **Proportion**: Size relationships between parts
- **Unity**: Coherence 와 harmony 의 whole composition
