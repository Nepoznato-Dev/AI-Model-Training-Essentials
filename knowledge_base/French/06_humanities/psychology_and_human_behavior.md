# # Developmental Psychologie

Developmental Psychologie examines how people change et grow throughout their lifespan. Prenatal Développement includes germinal, embryonic, et fetal stages. Infancy (0-2 years) features rapid physical growth, attachment formation, et sensorimotor learning. Childhood (2-12 years) involves Langue Développement, social skills, et concrete thinking. # # Social Psychologie

Social Psychologie studies how individuals think, feel, et behave dans social contexts. Conformity is adjusting behavior to match group norms, demonstrated dans Asch's line experiments. Obedience is complying avec authority figures, studied dans Milgram's shock experiments. Social facilitation improves Performance on simple tasks when others watch. Bystander effect reduces helping behavior when others are present. Aggression can be influenced by biology, frustration, et social learning. # # Personality Psychologie

Personality refers to enduring patterns de thoughts, feelings, et behaviors. le/la Big Five personality traits are openness (curiosity, creativity), conscientiousness (organization, dependability), extraversion (sociability, energy from others), agreeableness (cooperation, compassion), et neuroticism (emotional instability, anxiety). Personality develops through genetics, environment, et experiences. Assessment tools include self-report inventories (MMPI) et projective tests (Rorschach inkblots).

# # Mental Health et Disorders

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, et social well-being. Psychological disorders are patterns causing distress or impaired functioning. Anxiety disorders include generalized anxiety, panic disorder, phobias, et social anxiety. Mood disorders include depression (persistent sadness) et bipolar disorder (mood swings). Schizophrenia involves distorted thinking, perceptions, et emotions. Eating disorders include anorexia nervosa, bulimia, et binge-eating. Personality disorders involve inflexible patterns causing problems (e.g., borderline, antisocial). Trauma-related disorders include PTSD following traumatic Événements. le/la DSM-5 (Diagnostic et Statistical Manual) classifies mental disorders. Causes de disorders involve biological factors (genetics, brain chemistry), psychological factors (trauma, learned patterns), et social factors (stress, environment). # # Therapeutic Approaches

Psychotherapy involves treating mental disorders through psychological methods. Psychodynamic therapy explores unconscious conflicts et past experiences. Cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) changes negative thought patterns et behaviors. Humanistic therapy emphasizes self-exploration et personal growth. Behavioral therapy modifies behaviors through conditioning techniques. Family therapy addresses family dynamics affecting individuals. Group therapy provides peer support. Biomedical therapies include medications (antidepressants, antipsychotics, mood stabilizers) et procedures (ECT, TMS). Evidence-based practice integrates research evidence, clinical expertise, et patient values. Prevention et early intervention improve outcomes. Self-care practices include exercise, sleep, Nutrition, mindfulness, et social connection.

# # Motivation et Emotion

Motivation drives behavior toward goals. Intrinsic motivation comes from internal satisfaction; extrinsic motivation from external rewards. Maslow's hierarchy proposes needs from physiological to self-actualization. Drive-reduction theory suggests behavior aims to reduce internal tension. Instinct theory proposes innate behavioral patterns. Emotion involves physiological arousal, expressive behaviors, et conscious experience. Major emotion theories include James-Lange (emotion follows physiological response), Cannon-Bard (emotion et response occur simultaneously), et Schachter-Singer (emotion depends on interpretation de arousal). Basic emotions include happiness, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, et disgust. Emotional intelligence involves recognizing et managing emotions dans oneself et others. Stress triggers le/la fight-or-flight response; chronic stress harms health.

# # Learning et Conditioning

Learning is relatively permanent behavior change through experience. Classical conditioning (Pavlov) pairs neutral stimuli avec natural responses. Operant conditioning (Skinner) uses consequences to shape behavior. Positive reinforcement adds desirable stimuli to increase behavior; negative reinforcement removes aversive stimuli to increase behavior. Punishment decreases behavior. Observational learning (Bandura) occurs by watching others. Latent learning happens without immediate reinforcement. Insight learning involves sudden problem comprehension. Habituation reduces response to repeated stimuli. Generalization responds similarly to similar stimuli; discrimination distinguishes between stimuli. Extinction weakens conditioned responses when reinforcement stops.
