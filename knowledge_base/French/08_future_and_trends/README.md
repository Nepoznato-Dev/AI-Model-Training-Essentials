# Avenir et tendances
Une collection structurée de documents de référence couvrant les technologies émergentes, les changements sociétaux, les futurs spécifiques à un domaine et les cadres de prospective stratégique – explorant ce qui s'en vient et comment s'y préparer.
## Structure
```
08_future_and_trends/
├── README.md                          ← You are here
├── technology/                        ← Technology-driven futures
│   ├── emerging_technologies.md          Quantum computing, biotech, nanotechnology
│   ├── future_of_computing.md            Moore's Law, neuromorphic chips, edge computing
│   ├── ai_in_everyday_life.md            Smart assistants, recommendation systems, privacy
│   ├── climate_technology_and_green_innovation.md  Renewables, carbon capture, green hydrogen
│   └── space_exploration_roadmap.md      Space missions and timelines
├── society_and_domains/               ← Societal and domain-specific futures
│   ├── demographic_shifts.md             Population trends, migration, urbanization
│   ├── education_transformation.md       Online learning, AI tutoring
│   ├── future_of_work.md                 Automation, remote work, reskilling
│   ├── future_healthcare.md              Personalized medicine, AI diagnostics
│   ├── future_transportation.md          EVs, autonomous vehicles, hyperloop
│   └── sustainable_future.md             Climate, energy transition, circular economy
└── strategy/                          ← Foresight frameworks and geopolitics
    ├── scenario_planning.md              Future scenarios and frameworks

```
