# Psikoloji ve İnsan Davranışı

## Giriş to Psikoloji

Psikoloji is bu scientific study içinde mind ve behavior. It examines how people think, feel, act, ve interact. Major perspectives içinde Psikoloji include biological (examining brain structures ve neurotransmitters), behavioral (focusing on observable behaviors ve learning), cognitive (studying mental processes like memory ve decision-making), psychodynamic (exploring unconscious drives ve conflicts, originating ile Freud), humanistic (emphasizing personal growth ve self-actualization), ve social-cultural (examining how environment ve Kültür shape behavior). Psikoloji uses scientific methods including experiments, observations, surveys, ve case studies to understand human experience.

## Cognitive Psikoloji

Cognitive Psikoloji studies mental processes including attention, perception, memory, Dil, problem-solving, ve decision-making. Attention is bu ability to focus on specific stimuli while filtering out others. Perception involves organizing ve interpreting sensory information. Memory has three main stages: encoding (getting information içinde), storage (retaining information), ve retrieval (getting information out). Short-term memory holds limited information briefly; long-term memory has vast capacity için extended periods. Working memory temporarily manipulates information için complex tasks. Dil acquisition involves understanding ve producing İletişim. Problem-solving strategies include algorithms (step-by-step procedures) ve heuristics (mental shortcuts). Decision-making can be influenced by biases ve emotions.

## Developmental Psikoloji

Developmental Psikoloji examines how people change ve grow throughout their lifespan. Prenatal Geliştirme includes germinal, embryonic, ve fetal stages. Infancy (0-2 years) features rapid physical growth, attachment formation, ve sensorimotor learning. Childhood (2-12 years) involves Dil Geliştirme, social skills, ve concrete thinking. Adolescence (12-18 years) brings puberty, identity formation, ve abstract reasoning. Early adulthood (20s-30s) focuses on career, relationships, ve independence. Middle adulthood (40s-60s) often involves career consolidation ve caring için aging parents. Late adulthood (65+) may include retirement, health challenges, ve reflection. Key theories include Piaget's stages içinde cognitive Geliştirme, Erikson's psychosocial stages, ve Kohlberg's moral Geliştirme theory. Doğa (genetics) ve nurture (environment) both influence Geliştirme.

## Social Psikoloji

Social Psikoloji studies how individuals think, feel, ve behave içinde social contexts. Conformity is adjusting behavior to match group norms, demonstrated içinde Asch's line experiments. Obedience is complying ile authority figures, studied içinde Milgram's shock experiments. Social facilitation improves Performans on simple tasks when others watch. Social loafing reduces effort içinde groups. Groupthink occurs when groups prioritize harmony over critical evaluation. Prejudice is preconceived judgment about groups; discrimination is unequal treatment. Stereotypes are generalized beliefs about groups. Attribution theory explains how we interpret others' behavior (internal vs. external causes). bu fundamental attribution error overestimates personality ve underestimates situation when judging others. Bystander effect reduces helping behavior when others are present. Aggression can be influenced by biology, frustration, ve social learning. Attraction involves proximity, similarity, physical attractiveness, ve reciprocity.

## Personality Psikoloji

Personality refers to enduring patterns içinde thoughts, feelings, ve behaviors. bu Big Five personality traits are openness (curiosity, creativity), conscientiousness (organization, dependability), extraversion (sociability, energy from others), agreeableness (cooperation, compassion), ve neuroticism (emotional instability, anxiety). Freud's psychoanalytic theory proposed id (instincts), ego (reality), ve superego (morality). Humanistic theories (Maslow, Rogers) emphasize growth potential ve self-actualization. Trait theories identify consistent characteristics across situations. Type theories categorize people (e.g., Type A vs. Type B behavior patterns). Personality develops through genetics, environment, ve experiences. Assessment tools include self-report inventories (MMPI) ve projective tests (Rorschach inkblots).

## Mental Health ve Disorders

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, ve social well-being. Psychological disorders are patterns causing distress or impaired functioning. Anxiety disorders include generalized anxiety, panic disorder, phobias, ve social anxiety. Mood disorders include depression (persistent sadness) ve bipolar disorder (mood swings). Schizophrenia involves distorted thinking, perceptions, ve emotions. Eating disorders include anorexia nervosa, bulimia, ve binge-eating. Personality disorders involve inflexible patterns causing problems (e.g., borderline, antisocial). Trauma-related disorders include PTSD following traumatic Olaylar. bu DSM-5 (Diagnostic ve Statistical Manual) classifies mental disorders. Causes içinde disorders involve biological factors (genetics, brain chemistry), psychological factors (trauma, learned patterns), ve social factors (stress, environment). Stigma surrounding mental illness can prevent people from seeking help.

## Therapeutic Approaches

Psychotherapy involves treating mental disorders through psychological methods. Psychodynamic therapy explores unconscious conflicts ve past experiences. Cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) changes negative thought patterns ve behaviors. Humanistic therapy emphasizes self-exploration ve personal growth. Behavioral therapy modifies behaviors through conditioning techniques. Family therapy addresses family dynamics affecting individuals. Group therapy provides peer Destek. Biomedical therapies include medications (antidepressants, antipsychotics, mood stabilizers) ve procedures (ECT, TMS). Evidence-based practice integrates research evidence, clinical expertise, ve patient values. Prevention ve early intervention improve outcomes. Self-care practices include exercise, sleep, Beslenme, mindfulness, ve social connection.

## Neuroscience ve Behavior

Neuroscience examines bu nervous system's role içinde behavior. Neurons are nerve cells transmitting information through electrical ve chemical signals. Neurotransmitters are chemicals transmitting signals between neurons (dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine, GABA, glutamate). bu central nervous system includes brain ve spinal cord. bu peripheral nervous system connects CNS to body. bu brain's cerebrum handles higher functions; cerebellum coordinates movement; brainstem controls vital functions. bu limbic system processes emotions ve memory (amygdala, hippocampus, hypothalamus). Brain plasticity allows neural connections to change ile experience. Neuroimaging techniques (MRI, fMRI, PET scans) visualize brain structure ve activity. Hormones from bu endocrine system also influence behavior ve emotions.

## Motivation ve Emotion

Motivation drives behavior toward goals. Intrinsic motivation comes from internal satisfaction; extrinsic motivation from external rewards. Maslow's hierarchy proposes needs from physiological to self-actualization. Drive-reduction theory suggests behavior aims to reduce internal tension. Instinct theory proposes innate behavioral patterns. Emotion involves physiological arousal, expressive behaviors, ve conscious experience. Major emotion theories include James-Lange (emotion follows physiological response), Cannon-Bard (emotion ve response occur simultaneously), ve Schachter-Singer (emotion depends on interpretation içinde arousal). Basic emotions include happiness, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, ve disgust. Emotional intelligence involves recognizing ve managing emotions içinde oneself ve others. Stress triggers bu fight-or-flight response; chronic stress harms health.

## Learning ve Conditioning

Learning is relatively permanent behavior change through experience. Classical conditioning (Pavlov) pairs neutral stimuli ile natural responses. Operant conditioning (Skinner) uses consequences to shape behavior. Positive reinforcement adds desirable stimuli to increase behavior; negative reinforcement removes aversive stimuli to increase behavior. Punishment decreases behavior. Observational learning (Bandura) occurs by watching others. Latent learning happens without immediate reinforcement. Insight learning involves sudden problem comprehension. Habituation reduces response to repeated stimuli. Generalization responds similarly to similar stimuli; discrimination distinguishes between stimuli. Extinction weakens conditioned responses when reinforcement stops.
