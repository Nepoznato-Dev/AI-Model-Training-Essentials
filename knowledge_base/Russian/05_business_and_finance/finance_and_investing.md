# Финансы и Инвестирование

## Personal Финансы Основы

### Financial Planning Основы
- **Бюджетирование**: Track income и expenses, 50/30/20 rule (needs/wants/savings)
- **Резервный фонд**: 3-6 months из living expenses в liquid account
- **Чистый капитал**: Assets minus liabilities, track over time
- **Cash Flow Управление**: Money в vs. money out, positive cash flow essential
- **Финансовые цели**: Краткосрочный (<1 year), medium-term (1-5 years), long-term (5+ years)

### Banking Products
- **Текущие счета**: Daily transactions, debit cards, bill payments
- **Сберегательные счета**: Interest-bearing, limited withdrawals
- **Счета денежного рынка**: Higher interest, check-writing privileges
- **Certificates из Deposit (CDs)**: Fixed term, fixed rate, early withdrawal penalty
- **Высокодоходные сберегательные счета**: Online banks, competitive rates

### Credit и Debt
- **Кредитный рейтинг**: FICO (300-850), factors: payment История, utilization, length, mix, inquiries
- **Кредитные отчёты**: Annual free reports from Equifax, Experian, TransUnion
- **Types из Debt**: 
  - Полезный долг: Mortgage, student loans (potential ROI)
  - Плохой долг: Credit cards, payday loans (high interest)
- **Стратегии погашения долга**:
  - Лавина: Pay highest interest first (mathematically optimal)
  - Снежный ком: Pay smallest balance first (psychological wins)
- **Отношение долга к доходу**: Monthly debt payments / gross monthly income

## Investment Основы

### Asset Classes

#### Equities (Stocks)
- **Обыкновенные акции**: Ownership share, voting rights, dividends
- **Привилегированные акции**: Fixed dividends, priority в bankruptcy, no voting
- **Рыночная капитализация**: Large-cap, mid-cap, small-cap
- **Стили инвестирования**: Growth, value, blend, income
- **Секторы**: Технология, Здравоохранение, financials, consumer, industrial, energy, коммунальные услуги, materials, real estate, Коммуникация services

#### Fixed Income (Bonds)
- **Государственные облигации**: Treasury bills, notes, bonds (lowest risk)
- **Муниципальные облигации**: State/local government, tax-exempt interest
- **Корпоративные облигации**: Investment grade, high-yield/junk
- **Характеристики облигаций**: Face value, coupon rate, maturity, yield
- **Процентный риск**: Bond prices move inversely to rates
- **Кредитный риск**: Default probability, credit ratings (AAA to D)

#### Cash и Cash Equivalents
- **Казначейские векселя**: Краткосрочный government debt
- **Фонды денежного рынка**: Stable NAV, liquid
- **Коммерческие бумаги**: Краткосрочный corporate debt
- **Характеристики**: Low risk, low return, high liquidity

#### Real Estate
- **Прямое владение**: Physical properties, rental income
- **REITs**: Real Estate Investment Trusts, publicly traded
- **Краудфандинг недвижимости**: Platform-based property investment
- **Характеристики**: Income generation, appreciation, inflation hedge

#### Alternative Investments
- **Сырьевые товары**: Gold, silver, oil, agricultural products
- **Криптовалюты**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, digital assets
- **Частный капитал**: Direct company ownership, illiquid
- **Хедж-фонды**: Active Управление, diverse strategies
- **Коллекционные активы**: Art, wine, rare items

### Investment Vehicles

#### Mutual Funds
- Professionally managed portfolios
- Priced at end-из-day NAV
- Active or passive Управление
- Load vs. no-load funds
- Expense ratios vary widely

#### Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs)
- Trade like stocks throughout day
- Typically passive, index-tracking
- Lower expense ratios than mutual funds
- Tax-efficient structure
- Примеры: SPY (S&P 500), QQQ (Nasdaq), VTI (total market)

#### Index Funds
- Passive Управление tracking specific index
- Low costs, broad diversification
- Примеры: Total stock market, S&P 500, international

#### Annuities
- Insurance products providing income streams
- Fixed, variable, or indexed returns
- Immediate or deferred payout
- Tax-deferred growth

## Investment Strategies

### Portfolio Construction
- **Распределение активов**: Distribution across asset classes
- **Диверсификация**: Spread risk across securities, sectors, geographies
- **Современная портфельная теория**: Optimize risk-return through diversification
- **Эффективная граница**: Maximum return для given risk level
- **Корреляция**: How assets move relative to each other

### Investment Approaches
- **Passive Инвестирование**: Index funds, buy-и-hold, low costs
- **Active Инвестирование**: Stock picking, market timing, higher costs
- **Усреднение стоимости**: Invest fixed amounts regularly regardless из price
- **Value Инвестирование**: Buy undervalued securities (Warren Buffett approach)
- **Growth Инвестирование**: Focus on companies с above-average growth
- **Income Инвестирование**: Prioritize dividends и interest payments
- **ESG Инвестирование**: Экологический, social, governance factors

### Risk Управление
- **Толерантность к риску**: Ability и willingness to lose money
- **Инвестиционный горизонт**: When you need the money affects risk capacity
- **Размер позиции**: Don't concentrate too much в single investment
- **Стоп-лосс заявки**: Automatic sell at predetermined price
- **Ребалансировка**: Restore target allocation periodically
- **Хеджирование**: Options, inverse ETFs to reduce downside

## Retirement Planning

### Retirement Accounts (US)

#### Traditional IRA
- Tax-deductible contributions (income limits apply)
- Tax-deferred growth
- Taxed upon withdrawal
- Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) at age 73

#### Roth IRA
- After-tax contributions
- Tax-free growth и withdrawals
- No RMDs during lifetime
- Income limits для eligibility

#### 401(k) Plans
- Employer-sponsored retirement plans
- Pre-tax or Roth (after-tax) options
- Employer matching (free money)
- Higher contribution limits than IRAs
- Vesting schedules для employer contributions

#### Other Plans
- **403(b)**: Non-profit organizations
- **457**: Government employees
- **SEP IRA**: Self-employed, small Бизнес
- **Solo 401(k)**: Self-employed с no employees

### Retirement Planning Concepts
- **Коэффициент замещения**: 70-80% из pre-retirement income typically needed
- **Social Безопасность**: Government benefit, claim between 62-70
- **Пенсионные планы**: Defined benefit (employer guarantees payout)
- **Стратегии снятия средств**: 4% rule, dynamic spending, bucket strategy
- **Здравоохранение Costs**: Medicare, supplemental insurance, long-term care

## Market Mechanics

### Stock Exchanges
- **NYSE**: New York Stock Exchange, auction-based
- **Nasdaq**: Electronic exchange, Технология-heavy
- **Другие биржи**: London Stock Exchange, Tokyo Stock Exchange, Shanghai Stock Exchange

### Order Types
- **Рыночная заявка**: Execute immediately at current price
- **Лимитная заявка**: Execute only at specified price or better
- **Стоп-заявка**: Becomes market order when stop price reached
- **Stop-Лимитная заявка**: Becomes limit order when stop price reached
- **Трейлинг-стоп**: Stop price follows stock price movement

### Trading Concepts
- **Спред спроса и предложения**: Difference between buying и selling price
- **Объём торгов**: Number из shares traded
- **Ликвидность**: Ease из buying/selling without affecting price
- **Волатильность**: Price fluctuation magnitude
- **Короткая продажа**: Borrow shares, sell, hope to buy back cheaper
- **Маржинальная торговля**: Borrow money to invest, amplified gains/losses

### Market Indicators
- **S&P 500**: 500 largest US companies
- **Dow Jones Industrial Average**: 30 large blue-chip companies
- **Nasdaq Composite**: All Nasdaq-listed stocks, tech-heavy
- **Russell 2000**: Small-cap US stocks
- **VIX**: Волатильность index, "fear gauge"
- **Кривая доходности**: Bond yields across maturities, economic indicator

## Fundamental Analysis

### Financial Statements
- **Балансовый отчёт**: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity
- **Отчёт о прибылях и убытках**: Revenue - Expenses = Net Income
- **Отчёт о движении денежных средств**: Operating, Инвестирование, financing activities

### Key Ratios
- **Оценочные коэффициенты**:
  - P/E (Price-to-Earnings): Share price / earnings per share
  - P/B (Price-to-Book): Market value / book value
  - P/S (Price-to-Sales): Market cap / revenue
  - EV/EBITDA: Enterprise value / earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization

- **Коэффициенты прибыльности**:
  - Gross Margin: Gross profit / revenue
  - Operating Margin: Operating income / revenue
  - Net Margin: Net income / revenue
  - ROE (Return on Equity): Net income / shareholders' equity
  - ROA (Return on Assets): Net income / total assets

- **Ликвидность Ratios**:
  - Current Ratio: Current assets / current liabilities
  - Quick Ratio: (Current assets - inventory) / current liabilities

- **Коэффициенты долговой нагрузки**:
  - Debt-to-Equity: Total debt / shareholders' equity
  - Interest Coverage: EBIT / interest expense

### Qualitative Factors
- **Управление Quality**: Track record, alignment с shareholders
- **Конкурентное преимущество**: Moat, barriers to entry
- **Динамика отрасли**: Growth, competition, regulation
- **Корпоративное управление**: Board independence, shareholder rights

## Technical Analysis

### Chart Types
- **Линейные графики**: Closing prices over time
- **Столбиковые графики**: Open, high, low, close (OHLC)
- **Свечные графики**: Visual OHLC, bullish/bearish patterns

### Trend Analysis
- **Поддержка**: Price level where buying pressure exceeds selling
- **Сопротивление**: Price level where selling pressure exceeds buying
- **Линии тренда**: Connect highs or lows to show direction
- **Скользящие средние**: SMA (simple), EMA (exponential)
- **Золотой крест**: Short MA crosses above long MA (bullish)
- **Крест смерти**: Short MA crosses below long MA (bearish)

### Technical Indicators
- **Relative Strength Index (RSI)**: Momentum oscillator (0-100)
- **MACD**: Moving Average Convergence Divergence
- **Полосы Боллинджера**: Волатильность bands around moving average
- **Объём торгов Indicators**: On-Balance Объём торгов, volume-weighted average price
- **Уровни Фибоначчи**: Поддержка/resistance levels based on ratios

### Chart Patterns
- **Разворотные паттерны**: Head и shoulders, double top/bottom
- **Паттерны продолжения**: Triangles, flags, pennants
- **Пробои**: Price moves beyond defined range

## Tax Considerations

### Investment Taxes
- **Прирост капитала**: Profit from selling investments
  - Краткосрочный: Held <1 year, taxed as ordinary income
  - Долгосрочный: Held >1 year, preferential rates (0%, 15%, 20%)
- **Дивиденды**: 
  - Квалифицированные: Lower tax rates
  - Обычные: Taxed as income
- **Процентный доход**: Generally taxed as ordinary income
- **Налоговое зачётное закрытие убытков**: Sell losers to offset gains

### Tax-Advantaged Accounts
- **Traditional IRA/401(k)**: Tax deduction now, taxed later
- **Roth IRA/401(k)**: No deduction now, tax-free later
- **HSA (Health Savings Account)**: Triple tax advantage для medical expenses
- **529 Plans**: Tax-advantaged education savings

### Tax Efficiency Strategies
- Asset location (which accounts hold which investments)
- Hold investments long-term для preferential rates
- Use tax-efficient funds (ETFs, index funds)
- Consider municipal bonds для taxable accounts

## Economic Factors

### Macroeconomic Indicators
- **GDP**: Gross Domestic Product, economic growth
- **Инфляция**: CPI, PCE, purchasing power erosion
- **Безработица**: Labor market health
- **Процентные ставки**: Federal Reserve policy, borrowing costs
- **Потребительская уверенность**: Spending sentiment
- **Manufacturing Данные**: PMI, industrial production

### Federal Reserve и Monetary Policy
- **Двойной мандат**: Maximum employment, stable prices (2% inflation)
- **Инструменты**: Federal funds rate, open market operations, quantitative easing
- **Влияние**: Interest rates affect bonds, stocks, currency, economy

### Бизнес Cycle
- **Подъём**: Economic growth, rising employment
- **Пик**: Maximum growth, potential overheating
- **Спад/рецессия**: Economic decline, rising unemployment
- **Дно**: Bottom, recovery begins

## Behavioral Финансы

### Common Biases
- **Неприятие потерь**: Losses hurt more than equivalent gains please
- **Предвзятость подтверждения**: Seek information confirming existing beliefs
- **Чрезмерная самоуверенность**: Overestimate knowledge и abilities
- **Якорение**: Rely too heavily on first piece из information
- **Стадное мышление**: Follow crowd behavior
- **Предвзятость недавности**: overweight recent События
- **Эффект владения**: Value owned items more highly
- **Ошибка невозвратных затрат**: Continue based on past investment, not Будущее prospects

### Emotional Инвестирование Pitfalls
- Panic selling during downturns
- FOMO (Fear из Missing Out) buying at tops
- Trading too frequently
- Chasing past Производительность
- Timing the market (nearly impossible consistently)

### Лучшие практики
- Create и stick to investment plan
- Automate contributions
- Avoid checking portfolio too frequently
- Focus on controllables (savings rate, costs, asset allocation)
- Maintain long-term perspective

## Financial Advisors и Services

### Types из Advisors
- **Registered Investment Advisor (RIA)**: Fiduciary standard, fee-based
- **Broker-Dealer**: Suitability standard, commission-based
- **Робо-советники**: Automated Инвестирование, lower costs
- **Финансовый планировщик**: Comprehensive planning, may be fee-only or commission

### Compensation Models
- **Fee-Only**: Percentage из assets, hourly, flat fee (no commissions)
- **Комиссионная модель**: Paid on product sales
- **Fee-Based**: Combination из fees и commissions
- **Assets Under Управление (AUM)**: Typically 0.5%-1.5% annually

### Choosing an Advisor
- Verify credentials (CFP, CFA, CPA)
- Understand compensation structure
- Check regulatory История (SEC, FINRA BrokerCheck)
- Ensure fiduciary duty
- Assess Коммуникация style и compatibility
