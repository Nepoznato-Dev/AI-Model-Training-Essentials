## Purpose

These documents provide foundational skills that complement technical expertise:
- **Research Methods**: How to investigate questions systematically
- **Critical Thinking**: How to analyze arguments and avoid logical fallacies
- **Technical Writing**: How to communicate complex ideas clearly
- **Presentation Skills**: How to deliver impactful talks and demos
- **Career Development**: How to plan and advance your tech career
- **Problem Solving**: How to approach and solve complex problems
- **Time Management**: How to maximize productivity sustainably
