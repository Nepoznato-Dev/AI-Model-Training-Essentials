# Career Development

## Introduction

Career development in technology requires intentional planning, continuous learning, and strategic relationship building. This guide provides frameworks and strategies for advancing your tech career.

## Career Planning

### Career Stages in Tech

| Stage | Focus | Typical Duration |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| Learning | Building foundational skills | 0-2 years |
| Contributing | Delivering value independently | 2-5 years |
| Leading | Guiding others, architectural decisions | 5-10 years |
| Influencing | Strategy, organization-wide impact | 10+ years |

### Setting Career Goals

**Short-term (6-12 months)**:
- Master specific technology or skill
- Complete significant project
- Build portfolio piece
- Expand network by X contacts

**Mid-term (1-3 years)**:
- Reach next level/seniority
- Lead team or major initiative
- Develop area of expertise
- Increase compensation target

**Long-term (3-5+ years)**:
- Achieve desired role/title
- Build reputation in field
- Financial goals
- Work-life balance targets

### Career Paths in Technology

**Individual Contributor Track**:
Junior - Mid - Senior - Staff - Principal - Distinguished/Fellow

**Management Track**:
Senior - Tech Lead - Engineering Manager - Director - VP - CTO

**Specialist Track**:
- Security Engineer
- DevOps/SRE Specialist
- Data Scientist
- ML/AI Engineer
- UX Engineer

## Skill Development

### T-Shaped Skills Model

- **Horizontal bar**: Broad knowledge across domains
- **Vertical bar**: Deep expertise in one area

### Technical Skills Matrix

| Level | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Novice | Basic awareness | Heard of Kubernetes |
| Beginner | Can follow tutorials | Deploy simple app |
| Intermediate | Work independently | Design cluster architecture |
| Advanced | Teach others | Optimize performance, troubleshoot |
| Expert | Industry recognition | Contribute to project, speak at conferences |

### Learning Strategies

**70-20-10 Rule**:
- 70% from challenging assignments
- 20% from relationships/feedback
- 10% from formal training

**Deliberate Practice**:
- Focus on specific weaknesses
- Get immediate feedback
- Push beyond comfort zone
- Reflect and adjust

### Staying Current

**Weekly Habits**:
- Read industry newsletters
- Follow key influencers on social media
- Experiment with new tools
- Code side projects

**Monthly Habits**:
- Attend meetups or webinars
- Write blog post or documentation
- Review and update learning goals

**Quarterly Habits**:
- Take online course
- Attend conference
- Evaluate career progress

## Networking

**External Network**:

**Mentorship Network**:

- Share knowledge generously
- Make introductions between others

**Networking Venues**:
- Company events and meetings
- Industry conferences
- Meetups and user groups

### Maintaining Relationships

**Regular Touchpoints**:
- Quarterly check-ins with key contacts
- Congratulate on achievements
- Share relevant opportunities
- Request advice periodically

## Job Transitions

### When to Consider Moving

**Good Reasons**:
- Limited growth opportunities
- Compensation below market
- Misalignment with values
- Learning plateau

### Interview Preparation

**Technical Interviews**:
- Practice coding problems
- Review system design concepts
- Prepare project examples
- Mock interviews with peers

**Behavioral Interviews**:
- STAR method stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Leadership principles alignment
- Conflict resolution examples

### Negotiation

**Know Your Value**:
- Research market rates (Levels.fyi, Glassdoor)
- Understand total compensation (base, equity, bonus, benefits)
- Identify your unique value proposition

**Negotiation Tips**:
- Let them make first offer when possible
- Counter with data-backed reasoning
- Negotiate multiple elements, not just salary
- Get everything in writing

### Onboarding Success

**First 30 Days**:
- Learn team dynamics
- Understand systems and processes
- Build relationships
- Deliver small win

**First 90 Days**:
- Take ownership of component/project
- Establish credibility
- Set clear goals with manager

## Leadership Growth

### Leadership Competencies

**Technical Leadership**:
- Architecture decisions
- Code review and quality
- Technical strategy

**People Leadership**:
- Coaching and mentoring
- Feedback delivery
- Conflict resolution

**Strategic Leadership**:
- Business acumen
- Stakeholder management
- Vision setting

### Developing Leadership Skills

**Seek Opportunities**:
- Lead projects
- Mentor junior engineers
- Present to leadership

**Get Feedback**:
- 360-degree reviews
- Skip-level meetings
- Peer feedback

### Transitioning to Management

**Considerations**:
- Less hands-on coding
- More meetings and communication
- Success through others

**Preparation**:
- Shadow current managers
- Take on lead responsibilities
- Develop emotional intelligence

## Work-Life Integration

### Setting Boundaries

**Time Boundaries**:
- Defined work hours
- Break schedules
- Vacation time
- After-hours communication rules

**Physical Boundaries**:
- Dedicated workspace
- Ergonomic setup
- Screen time limits

### Preventing Burnout

**Warning Signs**:
- Chronic exhaustion
- Cynicism about work
- Reduced effectiveness

**Prevention Strategies**:
- Regular time off
- Hobbies outside work
- Exercise routine
- Social connections

Career development requires:

- Strong personal brand
- Thoughtful job transitions
- Leadership growth
