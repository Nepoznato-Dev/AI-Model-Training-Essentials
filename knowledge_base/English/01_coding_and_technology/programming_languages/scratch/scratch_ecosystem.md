## Summary

Scratch's ecosystem is designed for education and creative coding. The standard platform is: **Scratch 3.0** (online at scratch.mit.edu or **Scratch Desktop** for offline), **TurboWarp** for enhanced features and performance, and built-in **extensions** for music, drawing, and hardware. Scratch excels at teaching programming concepts to beginners (ages 8-16), creating interactive stories, games, and animations. The community has over 100 million shared projects. Scratch's strengths are visual block-based programming, immediate visual feedback, and a supportive community for learning.
