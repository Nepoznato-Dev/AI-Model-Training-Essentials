# Marketing and Digital Strategy

Marketing is how businesses create, communicate, and deliver value to customers. It encompasses the entire system of understanding customer needs, positioning a product to meet those needs, and building relationships that convert buyers into advocates. The digital revolution has transformed the tools, channels, and speed of marketing, while the fundamentals remain consistent.

---

## Marketing Fundamentals

### The Marketing Mix (4Ps)

The classic framework for thinking about any product's market strategy:

| P | Question It Answers | Key Decisions |
|---|---------------------|---------------|
| **Product** | What are we selling? | Features, quality, branding, packaging, warranties |
| **Price** | What should it cost? | List price, discounts, payment terms, subscription vs one-time |
| **Place** | Where can customers buy it? | Distribution channels, retail, e-commerce, direct-to-consumer |
| **Promotion** | How will people find out about it? | Advertising, PR, social media, content, events |

### Extended Mix (7Ps — for services)

| P | Addition | Example |
|---|----------|---------|
| **People** | Staff, culture, customer service | Zappos' legendary support |
| **Process** | How the service is delivered | McDonald's standardised operations |
| **Physical Evidence** | The environment and tangibles | Apple Store design and layout |

### Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning (STP)

| Step | What You Do | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Segment** | Divide the market into distinct groups | By demographics, behaviour, geography, psychographics |
| **Target** | Choose which segments to serve | Tesla initially targeted affluent early adopters |
| **Position** | Define how you want to be perceived in the target's mind | Volvo = safety; Apple = design and simplicity |

### The Marketing Funnel

The journey from stranger to loyal customer:

| Stage | Goal | Typical Metrics |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| **Awareness** | They learn you exist | Impressions, reach, website visits |
| **Interest** | They want to know more | Time on site, content downloads, email signups |
| **Consideration** | They evaluate you against alternatives | Demo requests, comparison page visits, free trials |
| **Conversion** | They buy | Sales, conversion rate, revenue |
| **Retention** | They come back | Repeat purchase rate, churn rate, NPS |
| **Advocacy** | They tell others | Referrals, reviews, social shares |

## Digital Marketing Channels

### Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **On-page SEO** | Title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, keyword placement, internal linking |
| **Technical SEO** | Site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, structured data (schema), HTTPS |
| **Off-page SEO** | Backlinks, domain authority, social signals, brand mentions |
| **Content SEO** | Creating content that answers search intent (informational, navigational, transactional) |

**Key tools**: Google Search Console, Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz, Screaming Frog.

### Search Engine Marketing (SEM) / Paid Search

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **PPC (Pay-Per-Click)** | You pay each time someone clicks your ad |
| **Quality Score** | Google's rating of ad relevance, landing page, and expected CTR — affects cost and position |
| **Long-tail keywords** | Longer, more specific phrases with less competition and higher intent |
| **Negative keywords** | Words that prevent your ad from showing (reduces wasted spend) |

**Key platforms**: Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising (Bing).

### Content Marketing

| Content Type | Purpose | Best For |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Blog posts** | SEO traffic; educate and build trust | Long-term organic growth |
| **Videos** | Engagement; explain complex topics | YouTube, social media |
| **Podcasts** | Build authority; reach audiences on the go | Thought leadership |
| **Whitepapers / Ebooks** | Generate leads (gated content) | B2B marketing |
| **Infographics** | Visual data storytelling; shareable | Social media, backlinks |
| **Case studies** | Prove results; reduce purchase anxiety | Sales enablement, B2B |
| **Newsletters** | Nurture existing audience; stay top of mind | Retention, community |

### Social Media Marketing

| Platform | Audience | Best Content |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Instagram** | 18–34; visual | Photos, Reels, Stories |
| **TikTok** | Gen Z; short video | Authentic, trend-driven content |
| **LinkedIn** | Professionals; B2B | Thought leadership, company news |
| **X (Twitter)** | News, tech, politics | Short-form, real-time engagement |
| **YouTube** | All ages; long-form | Tutorials, reviews, entertainment |
| **Facebook** | 25+; broad | Groups, events, targeted ads |
| **Pinterest** | Visual discovery; shopping | DIY, recipes, fashion, home |

### Email Marketing

| Metric | Benchmark | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Open rate** | 15–25% | Subject line effectiveness |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | 2–5% | Content relevance and CTA strength |
| **Conversion rate** | 1–5% | Overall campaign effectiveness |
| **Unsubscribe rate** | <0.5% | List health and content fit |
| **Revenue per email** | Varies | Bottom-line impact |

**Best practices**: Segment your list. Personalise subject lines. Test send times. Automate welcome sequences. Never buy email lists.

## Analytics and Key Metrics

### The Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Formula | What It Means |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) | Total marketing spend ÷ new customers | How much it costs to acquire one customer |
| **CLV** (Customer Lifetime Value) | Average revenue per customer × gross margin × avg customer lifespan | Total value a customer generates over time |
| **ROAS** (Return on Ad Spend) | Revenue from ads ÷ ad spend | Is advertising profitable? |
| **Conversion Rate** | Conversions ÷ total visitors × 100 | How effectively you turn visitors into customers |
| **Churn Rate** | Lost customers ÷ total customers × 100 | How many customers you're losing |
| **NPS** (Net Promoter Score) | % Promoters − % Detractors | Customer satisfaction and loyalty (-100 to +100) |

### The CAC:CLV Ratio

The single most important ratio for sustainable growth:

| Ratio | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **CLV:CAC < 1:1** | Losing money on every customer. Unsustainable. |
| **CLV:CAC ≈ 3:1** | Healthy. Industry benchmark for SaaS. |
| **CLV:CAC > 5:1** | Possibly underinvesting in growth. Could spend more on acquisition. |

### Analytics Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Google Analytics 4** | Web analytics; traffic, conversions, user behaviour |
| **Google Tag Manager** | Deploy tracking tags without developer involvement |
| **Mixpanel / Amplitude** | Product analytics; event-based tracking |
| **Hotjar / FullStory** | Behavioural analytics; heatmaps, session recordings |
| **HubSpot / Salesforce** | CRM; track leads through the funnel |
| **Looker / Tableau** | Data visualisation and business intelligence |

## Brand Strategy

A brand is not a logo. A brand is the sum of every interaction someone has with your company — what they expect, how they feel, what they tell their friends.

### Brand Elements

| Element | Purpose | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Brand purpose** | Why you exist beyond making money | Patagonia: "We're in business to save the planet" |
| **Brand values** | Principles that guide behaviour | Transparency, sustainability, innovation |
| **Brand positioning** | How you're different and better in your category | "The fastest", "the simplest", "the most reliable" |
| **Brand voice** | How you communicate (tone, style, vocabulary) | Nike: inspirational; Wendy's: witty and provocative |
| **Visual identity** | Logo, colours, typography, imagery style | Coca-Cola red; Apple minimalism |

### Competitive Analysis Framework

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1. Identify competitors** | Direct (same product), indirect (same problem, different solution), potential (could enter your market) |
| **2. Analyse their positioning** | What message are they sending? Who are they targeting? |
| **3. Map their offerings** | Features, pricing, channels, customer experience |
| **4. Identify gaps** | What are they not doing? Where are customers underserved? |
| **5. Define your differentiation** | What can you own that they can't easily copy? |

## Growth and Performance Marketing

### Growth Hacking

Growth hacking is the intersection of marketing, product, and data — focused on rapid, scalable user growth, typically in startups.

| Strategy | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Viral loops** | Product usage naturally invites others | Dropbox: invite a friend, get free storage |
| **Product-led growth** | The product sells itself through free tiers | Slack, Zoom, Notion |
| **Waitlists** | Create scarcity and FOMO | Clubhouse, Superhuman |
| **Integration as growth** | Embed your product in popular platforms | PayPal on eBay; Shopify on social media |
| **Content flywheel** | Content drives traffic → users → more content ideas → more traffic | HubSpot's blog → free tools → paid product |

### A/B Testing in Marketing

| Element to Test | Impact Area |
|----------------|-------------|
| Headlines and ad copy | Click-through rate |
| Landing page layout | Conversion rate |
| Email subject lines | Open rate |
| CTA button colour and text | Click rate |
| Pricing page structure | Revenue per visitor |
| Onboarding flow | Activation rate |

## Summary

Marketing is fundamentally about understanding people — their needs, concerns, and motivations. The digital toolkit has evolved considerably, but the fundamentals remain: understand the customer, deliver value, communicate effectively, measure outcomes, and iterate. Effective marketing aligns so closely with customer needs that it is experienced as assistance rather than promotion.
