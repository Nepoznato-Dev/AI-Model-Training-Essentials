# Humanities and Arts

A structured collection of reference documents covering history, arts, language, philosophy, psychology, and religion — the core disciplines of the humanities that provide context for understanding human civilization, creative expression, and critical thought.

## Structure

```

├── README.md                                    ← You are here
├── arts/                                        ← Creative expression
│   ├── literature.md                               Literary genres, movements, devices

│   ├── performing_arts.md                          Theater, film, dance
│   └── music_theory_and_acoustics.md               Music theory, harmony, acoustics
├── history/                                     ← Historical studies
│   ├── history_and_culture.md                      World history from ancient to modern
│   └── geography_and_geopolitics.md                Physical/human geography, political systems
├── language/                                    ← Language and linguistics
│   ├── language_and_english.md                     English grammar, usage, style
│   └── linguistics_and_language_science.md         Phonetics, syntax, semantics
├── philosophy_and_mind/                         ← Thought and behavior
│   ├── philosophy_and_critical_thinking.md         Philosophical traditions, reasoning

```
