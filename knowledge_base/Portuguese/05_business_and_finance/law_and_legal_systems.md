# Direito e Jurídico Sistemas

## Jurídico Sistemas Visão geral

A Jurídico system is o/a framework de rules, institutions, e procedures that govern a society. o/a main Jurídico Sistemas worldwide include common Direito (based on judicial precedents, used em UK, US, Canada, Australia), civil Direito (based on codified statutes, used em most de Europe, Latin America, Japan), religious Direito (based on religious texts, such as Islamic Sharia Direito), e customary Direito (based on traditional practices). Common Direito Sistemas rely heavily on case Direito e o/a principle de stare decisis (precedent must be followed). Civil Direito Sistemas use comprehensive Jurídico codes that judges apply to cases.

## Branches de Direito

Direito is typically divided into public Direito e private Direito. Public Direito governs relationships between individuals e o/a state, including constitutional Direito, administrative Direito, e criminal Direito. Private Direito (civil Direito) governs relationships between individuals, including contract Direito, tort Direito, property Direito, e family Direito. Criminal Direito deals com offenses against society punishable by o/a state. Civil Direito addresses disputes between parties where remedies typically involve compensation rather than punishment.

## Constitutional Direito

Constitutional Direito concerns o/a interpretation e implementation de a country's constitution. A constitution is o/a supreme Direito establishing o/a structure de government, distribution de powers, e fundamental rights. Judicial review allows courts to determine whether laws or government actions violate o/a constitution. o/a separation de powers divides government into legislative (makes laws), executive (enforces laws), e judicial (interprets laws) branches. Checks e balances prevent any one branch from becoming too powerful. Fundamental rights often include freedom de speech, religion, assembly, due process, e equal protection under o/a Direito.

## Contract Direito

A contract is a legally binding agreement between two or more parties. Essential elements de a valid contract include offer (a clear proposal), acceptance (agreement to o/a terms), consideration (something de value exchanged), capacity (Jurídico ability to contract), e legality (purpose must be Jurídico). Breach de contract occurs when a party fails to fulfill obligations. Remedies para breach include damages (monetary compensation), specific Desempenho (court orders fulfillment), or cancellation. Contracts can be written, oral, or implied by conduct, though certain types must be em writing under o/a Statute de Frauds.

## Tort Direito

Tort Direito addresses civil wrongs that cause harm or loss, resulting em Jurídico liability. Negligence is o/a failure to exercise reasonable care, causing injury to another. To prove negligence, a plaintiff must show: duty de care existed, breach de that duty, causation, e damages. Intentional torts include assault, battery, false imprisonment, defamation (libel e slander), e trespass. Strict liability applies regardless de fault em certain cases (defective products, dangerous activities). Damages em tort cases compensate para medical expenses, lost wages, pain e suffering, e sometimes punitive damages to punish egregious conduct.

## Property Direito

Property Direito governs ownership e use de real property (land e buildings) e personal property (movable items). Real property rights include possession, control, exclusion, enjoyment, e disposition. Types de ownership include fee simple (absolute ownership), life estate (ownership para lifetime), e leasehold (temporary possession). Landlord-tenant Direito regulates rental agreements. Intellectual property is a form de intangible property protecting creations de o/a mind. Eminent domain allows governments to take private property para public use com just compensation. Zoning laws regulate land use em specific areas.

## Criminal Direito e Procedure

Criminal Direito defines crimes e punishments. Crimes are classified as felonies (serious offenses punishable by imprisonment over one year) or misdemeanors (less serious offenses). Elements de a crime typically include actus reus (guilty act) e mens rea (guilty mind/intent). o/a presumption de innocence means defendants are innocent until proven guilty beyond a reasonable doubt. Constitutional protections em criminal proceedings include protection against unreasonable searches e seizures, right to counsel, right to a speedy e public trial, protection against self-incrimination, e protection against double jeopardy. Sentencing may involve imprisonment, fines, probation, or capital punishment (em some jurisdictions).

## Family Direito

Family Direito deals com domestic relations e family matters. Marriage is a Jurídico union creating rights e obligations between spouses. Divorce (dissolution de marriage) involves division de assets, spousal Suporte (alimony), e child custody. Child custody determines where children live e who makes decisions about their welfare. Child Suporte is financial Suporte paid by non-custodial parents. Adoption creates a Jurídico parent-child relationship. Domestic violence laws protect family members from abuse. Paternity establishes Jurídico fatherhood. Prenuptial agreements specify asset division if marriage ends.

## Intellectual Property

Intellectual property (IP) protects creations de o/a mind. Patents grant inventors exclusive rights to make, use, or sell inventions para a limited period (typically 20 years). Employment Direito governs relationships between employers e employees. At-will employment allows termination by either party at any time (common em o/a US). ## International Direito

International Direito governs relations between nations e international entities. Public international Direito includes treaties, customs, e principles binding on states. o/a United Nations Charter is a foundational treaty establishing o/a UN system. International humanitarian Direito (laws de war) regulates armed conflict e protects non-combatants. Human rights Direito establishes universal rights inherent to all humans. ## Jurídico Process e Courts

Civil procedure governs how civil lawsuits are conducted. A lawsuit begins when a plaintiff files a complaint. o/a defendant responds com an answer. Discovery is o/a pre-trial exchange de information. Motions are requests para court rulings. Trial involves presenting evidence e arguments to a judge or jury. Alternative dispute resolution includes mediation (neutral facilitator helps parties reach agreement) e arbitration (neutral third party makes binding decision). Class action lawsuits allow groups com similar claims to sue collectively.

Jurídico ethics govern lawyer conduct. Attorneys must maintain client confidentiality (attorney-client privilege). Conflicts de interest must be avoided or disclosed.
