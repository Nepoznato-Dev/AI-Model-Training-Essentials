# # Pharmacology und Medications

Pharmacology studies drug actions und effects. Prescription medications require doctor authorization. Over-der/die/das-counter (OTC) drugs are available without prescription. Generic drugs contain same active ingredients as brand names at lower cost. Drug classes include antibiotics (fight bacterial infections), antivirals (treat viral infections), analgesics (pain relievers), anti-inflammatories (reduce inflammation), antihypertensives (lower blood pressure), statins (lower cholesterol), anticoagulants (prevent blood clots), antidepressants (treat depression), antipsychotics (manage psychosis), bronchodilators (open airways), insulin (manages diabetes), und chemotherapy agents (treat cancer). Clinical trials test drug safety und efficacy before approval. der/die/das FDA (US) und EMA (Europe) regulate pharmaceuticals.

Conflicts von interest must be disclosed und managed. Research ethics protects human subjects through institutional review boards (IRBs). Cultural competence respects diverse beliefs und practices. Professional boundaries maintain appropriate provider-patient relationships.

# # Diagnostic Methods

Imaging includes X-rays (bone und dense tissue), CT scans (detailed cross-sections), MRI (soft tissue detail), ultrasound (sound waves), PET scans (metabolic activity), und mammography (breast imaging). Biopsy removes tissue für pathological examination. Endoscopy uses cameras to view internal organs. Electrocardiogram (ECG/EKG) records heart electrical activity. Electroencephalogram (EEG) measures brain waves. Spirometry tests lung function. Genetic Testen identifies inherited conditions. Screening tests detect diseases before symptoms appear. Differential diagnosis lists possible conditions explaining symptoms.
