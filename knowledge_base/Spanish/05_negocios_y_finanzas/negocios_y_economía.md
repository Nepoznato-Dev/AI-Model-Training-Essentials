# Negocios y Economía

## Corporate Structures

A corporation is a Legal entity separate from its owners, providing limited liability protection to shareholders. el main types de Negocios structures incluyen sole proprietorships (owned by one person), partnerships (owned by two or more people), limited liability companies (LLCs), y corporations (C-corp, S-corp). Corporations are governed by a board de directors elected by shareholders. el chief executive officer (CEO) is el highest-ranking executive, responsible para making major corporate decisions. Other C-suite executives incluyen el Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), y Chief Tecnología Officer (CTO).

## Marketing Fundamentos

Marketing is el process de promoting, selling, y distributing products or services. el marketing mix consists del 4 Ps: Product (what you sell), Price (how much you charge), Place (where you sell it), y Promotion (how you advertise it). Market segmentation divides customers into groups based on demographics, Geografía, behavior, or psychographics. Brand identity is el visible elements de a brand (logo, colors, design) that distinguish it from competitors. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) is el expense incurred to acquire a new customer. Customer lifetime value (CLV) estimates el total revenue a Negocios can expect from a single customer account.

## Accounting Conceptos básicos

Accounting is el systematic recording, reporting, y analysis de financial transactions. el fundamental accounting equation is: Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Assets are resources owned by a Negocios (cash, inventory, equipment). Liabilities are obligations or debts owed to others. Equity represents el owner's claim on assets after liabilities are deducted. el balance sheet shows a company's financial position at a specific point en time. el income statement (profit y loss statement) shows revenues, expenses, y profits over a period. Cash flow statements track el movement de cash en y out de a Negocios. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) y International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) are el main accounting frameworks.

## Inversión y Finanzas

Inversión involves allocating money con el expectation de generating income or profit. Stocks represent ownership shares en a corporation. Bonds are debt securities where investors loan money to entities (corporations or governments) para a fixed interest rate. Mutual funds pool money from many investors to purchase a diversified portfolio de securities. Exchange-traded funds (ETFs) are similar to mutual funds but trade on stock exchanges like individual stocks. Diversification spreads investments across different assets to reduce risk. Compound interest is interest calculated on both el initial principal y accumulated interest. el risk-return tradeoff states that potential return rises con increased risk. Blue-chip stocks are shares en large, well-established companies con reliable Rendimiento. Dividends are payments made by corporations to shareholders from profits.

## Economic Indicators

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) measures el total value de goods y services produced en a country. Inflation is el rate at which prices rise over time, reducing purchasing power. el Consumer Price Index (CPI) measures changes en el price level de consumer goods y services. Unemployment rate is el percentage del labor force that is jobless y actively seeking employment. Interest rates, set by central banks, influence borrowing costs y economic activity. el Federal Reserve (US), European Central Bank (ECB), y Bank de England are major central banks. Recession is typically defined as two consecutive quarters de negative GDP growth. Bull markets indicate rising stock prices; bear markets indicate falling prices.

## Supply Chain y Operations

Supply chain Gestión oversees el flow de goods y services from raw materials to final products. Logistics involves el coordination de moving resources from origin to consumption. Inventory Gestión balances having enough stock to meet demand without excess holding costs. Just-en-time (JIT) manufacturing minimizes inventory by receiving goods only as needed. Quality control ensures products meet specified standards. Six Sigma is a methodology para eliminating defects y improving processes. Lean manufacturing se centra en minimizing waste while maximizing productivity.

## Entrepreneurship y Startups

Entrepreneurship is el process de designing, launching, y running a new Negocios. A startup is a young company founded to develop a unique product or service. Venture capital is funding provided by investors to startups con high growth potential. Angel investors are affluent individuals who provide capital para startups, often en exchange para equity. Crowdfunding raises small amounts de money from many people via online platforms. A minimum viable product (MVP) is a version de a product con just enough features to satisfy early customers. Pivot refers to a fundamental change en Negocios strategy. Scalability is el ability to grow without being hampered by structure or resources.

## International Trade

International trade involves el exchange de goods y services across borders. Imports are goods brought into a country; exports are goods sent out. A trade deficit occurs when imports exceed exports; a trade surplus is el opposite. Tariffs are taxes on imported goods. Free trade agreements reduce barriers between countries. el World Trade Organization (WTO) regulates international trade. Currency exchange rates affect el relative cost de imports y exports. Globalization has increased interconnectedness de economies worldwide.

## Negocios Ethics y Corporate Social Responsibility

Negocios ethics involves moral principles guiding Negocios behavior. Corporate social responsibility (CSR) is a company's commitment to operate sustainably y contribute positively to society. Ambiental, Social, y Governance (ESG) criteria evaluate company practices on Sostenibilidad y ethical impact. Stakeholders incluyen anyone affected by a company's actions (employees, customers, communities, shareholders). Ethical dilemmas arise when Negocios decisions conflict con moral values. Transparency y accountability are key principles de ethical Negocios practice.
