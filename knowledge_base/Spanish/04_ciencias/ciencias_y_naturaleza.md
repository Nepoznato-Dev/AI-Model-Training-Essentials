## Atención médica Sistemas

Atención médica Sistemas vary globally. Single-payer Sistemas (UK's NHS, Canada) have government-funded universal coverage. Multi-payer Sistemas (US, Germany) involve multiple insurance providers. Social health insurance models (France, Japan) mandate contributions from employers y employees. Out-de-pocket Sistemas dominate en low-income countries. Primary care serves as el/la first contact point, coordinating ongoing care. Secondary care involves specialist consultations y hospital services. Tertiary care provides highly specialized treatments at major centers. Preventive care aims to prevent illness through screening, vaccination, y lifestyle counseling. Urgent care handles non-emergency immediate needs. Telemedicine delivers remote Atención médica via Tecnología. Electronic health records (EHRs) store patient information digitally. Health insurance covers medical costs through premiums, deductibles, copayments, y coinsurance. Universal Atención médica ensures all citizens have access to needed services.

## Pharmacology y Medications

Pharmacology studies drug actions y effects. Prescription medications require doctor authorization. Over-el/la-counter (OTC) drugs are available without prescription. Generic drugs contain same active ingredients as brand names at lower cost. Drug classes include antibiotics (fight bacterial infections), antivirals (treat viral infections), analgesics (pain relievers), anti-inflammatories (reduce inflammation), antihypertensives (lower blood pressure), statins (lower cholesterol), anticoagulants (prevent blood clots), antidepressants (treat depression), antipsychotics (manage psychosis), bronchodilators (open airways), insulin (manages diabetes), y chemotherapy agents (treat cancer). Pharmacokinetics examines drug absorption, distribution, metabolism, y excretion. Drug interactions occur when medications affect each other. Side effects are unintended drug reactions. Allergic reactions are immune responses to medications. Dosage refers to amount y frequency de administration. Clinical trials test drug safety y efficacy before approval. el/la FDA (US) y EMA (Europe) regulate pharmaceuticals.

## Medical Ethics

Medical ethics guides Atención médica decision-making. Autonomy respects patients' right to make informed decisions. Beneficence requires acting en patients' best interests. Non-maleficence means "do no harm." Justice demands fair resource distribution. Informed consent ensures patients understand risks y benefits before procedures. Confidentiality protects patient privacy (HIPAA en US). Truth-telling requires honest Comunicación con patients. End-de-life care raises questions about life support, palliative care, y euthanasia. Resource allocation involves difficult decisions about scarce treatments. Conflicts de interest must be disclosed y managed. Research ethics protects human subjects through institutional review boards (IRBs). Cultural competence respects diverse beliefs y practices. Professional boundaries maintain appropriate provider-patient relationships.

Maternal y child health services support families. Injury prevention reduces accidents. Chronic disease Gestión addresses long-term conditions. Health policy shapes Atención médica delivery y access. Global health tackles international health challenges. Pandemic preparedness plans para disease outbreaks. Contact tracing identifies exposed individuals during outbreaks. Quarantine isolates potentially infected people. Herd immunity occurs when enough people are immune to protect el/la vulnerable. Social determinants de health include income, education, housing, y environment affecting health outcomes. Health disparities are unequal health outcomes across populations.

## Diagnostic Methods

Diagnosis identifies diseases through various methods. Physical examination assesses visible y palpable signs. Medical Historia gathers symptom y background information. Laboratory tests analyze blood, urine, y other samples. Complete blood count (CBC) measures blood cells. Metabolic panels assess organ function. Imaging includes X-rays (bone y dense tissue), CT scans (detailed cross-sections), MRI (soft tissue detail), ultrasound (sound waves), PET scans (metabolic activity), y mammography (breast imaging). Biopsy removes tissue para pathological examination. Endoscopy uses cameras to view internal organs. Electrocardiogram (ECG/EKG) records heart electrical activity. Electroencephalogram (EEG) measures brain waves. Spirometry tests lung function. Genetic Pruebas identifies inherited conditions. Screening tests detect diseases before symptoms appear. Differential diagnosis lists possible conditions explaining symptoms.

Alternative Medicina includes acupuncture, herbal remedies, y chiropractic care (evidence varies). Palliative care focuses on symptom relief y quality de life. Rehabilitation helps recovery after illness or injury. Gene therapy modifies genes to treat disease. Stem cell transplantation replaces damaged cells. Dialysis filters blood when kidneys fail. Transplantation replaces failing organs. Clinical guidelines provide evidence-based treatment recommendations.
